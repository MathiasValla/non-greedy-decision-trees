prior work rather than a new induction algorithm. The accompanying response

score records, reconstructs the inner-validation winners, and applies the
complete eleven-contrast multiplicity adjustment. The expanded selected family
has higher estimated mean accuracy than selected RF, but its heterogeneous
effects, benchmark sensitivities and much larger computation qualify the
practical interpretation. Its choices include pure forests; the conclusion
does not attribute the entire gain to sparse mixing or claim equivalence.
