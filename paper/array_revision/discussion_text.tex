The revision changes the interpretation of the exploratory result. Sparse two-sighted replacements can improve a constrained shallow CART ensemble, including under the stated family and constructed-task sensitivities. That is a restricted configuration effect, not a generally improved accuracy--time frontier. Its median gain is smaller than its mean gain, and the extra cost is substantial even when only a minority of members use bounded search. With deeper members, mean gains attenuate and the adjusted signed-rank analysis does not establish the same location advantage. Uniform two-sighted induction can be detrimental at unrestricted depth. Better local terminal training impurity is therefore not a certificate of better prediction.

This interpretation is consistent with studying shallow members as a regularized regime, rather than dismissing them as inherently unrealistic. The cited tree-size literature motivates conditional capacity control, not one universally preferred depth. Our training-selected forests use both shallow and deeper configurations. Depth-matched comparisons answer whether stronger local optimization helps within a capacity constraint; comparisons with tuned conventional learners answer a different, more practical question. On the present sample, the latter do not favour the fixed mixtures. Neither a nonsignificant contrast nor the success of a restricted shallow ensemble warrants the assertion that greedy induction is equivalent to every non-greedy method.
