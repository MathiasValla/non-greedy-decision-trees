The completed evaluation contains 57 datasets, five partitions per dataset, and three final-depth settings, with 855 complete tasks in each of the two stages and 16,245 model score records. No planned dataset or repeat is removed. Five singleton-class partitions of \texttt{lymphography} use the disclosed inner-CV fallback. The main outcomes materially narrow the original claim:
\begin{itemize}
\item At depth three, the 100-tree CART ensemble with ten two-sighted replacements gains 1.07 accuracy percentage points [0.52, 1.70] over paired CART bagging ($p_H=0.0033$). The median gain is 0.20 points; there are 31 dataset-mean wins, 15 ties, and 11 losses. Mean constituent fitting cost rises by a factor of 11.6. This is not an equal-time advantage.
\item The shallow effect remains positive under equal weighting of the 48 known dataset families (0.82 points [0.32, 1.42], family-adjusted $p_H=0.018$) and on the 38 tasks outside the named constructed/synthetic set (0.60 points [0.14, 1.15], unadjusted interval). These are benchmark sensitivities, not evidence of population-wide generality.
\item At depth six and unrestricted depth, mean CART-mixture gains shrink to 0.48 and 0.34 points. Both medians are zero and both adjusted signed-rank $p$-values are 0.273. Positive unadjusted mean intervals and non-rejected signed-rank tests concern different estimands; neither establishes equivalence.
\item Uniform two-sighted induction performs worse at unrestricted depth: the single-tree difference from CART is $-5.14$ points [$-8.70$, $-1.86$] ($p_H=0.012$), and the pure 20-tree difference from the custom greedy ensemble is $-6.21$ points [$-9.59$, $-3.22$] ($p_H<0.001$). Search horizon remains two; it is not set equal to the unrestricted final depth.
\item Training-selected conventional forests have higher mean accuracy than the fixed CART mixtures at all final-depth settings. The mixture deficits are 5.27, 1.83, and 1.13 points, with tuned-family adjusted $p$-values below 0.001, 0.021, and 0.036. Their refits are cheaper, but tuning itself adds computation; the manuscript reports selection cost and makes no equal-total-budget claim.
\end{itemize}
Tables 1--2 and Figures 1--2 replace the original single-split argument. Complete records and reproducibility documentation support the new results. The abstract and conclusion now state a conditional shallow benefit and explicitly reject a general practical-superiority interpretation.
