\begin{itemize}
\item $k$-sighted trees score splits through bounded descendant subtrees.
\item Uniform extra sight gives modest gains at large fitting-time cost.
\item Sparse $k=2$ trees can improve mostly greedy bootstrap forests.
\item At a 0.7 s budget, $\bar{k}=1.25$ beats lower-sight forests.
\item Mixed forests expose a useful sparse-sight accuracy--time regime.
\end{itemize}
