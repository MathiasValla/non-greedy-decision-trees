Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Sparse Farther-Sighted Trees in
Shallow Classification Forests: A Short Note", for publication in the Journal
of Classification as a Short Note.

The note studies a bounded non-greedy variant of decision-tree induction in
which candidate splits are evaluated through optimized local descendant
subtrees. The main result is intentionally narrow: uniformly increasing sight
depth gives only small and inconsistent accuracy improvements relative to its
computational cost, while sparse farther-sighted trees can improve the
accuracy-time tradeoff of mostly greedy bootstrap forests.

The submission is original work, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has
not been previously published. The manuscript is prepared as an anonymized,
double-spaced, single-column review copy. Author information is supplied
separately on the title page, consistent with the journal's double-blind review
process.
